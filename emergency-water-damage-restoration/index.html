Maintenance Tips for Emergency Water Damage Restoration

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Before anything goes wrong, store the phone number for an emergency restoration crew.
  • Learn the location of your breaker panel, which lets you safely kill power to a wet room.
  • Find your main water shutoff today and make sure everyone in the house can close it.
  • Aging supply lines to toilets, faucets and washing machines should be swapped out before one fails.
  • Put leak alarms beneath sinks and beside the dishwasher, washer and water heater.
  • In basements and other low areas, keep documents and valuables up on shelves.

Warning Signs for Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Floor-level power strips, plugged-in appliances or the breaker panel are now wet.
  • An outlet, a light fixture or the ceiling above has water dripping out of it.
  • Pooled water is creeping over the floor into neighboring rooms or onto the staircase.
  • A leak upstairs is sending water down the walls of the level beneath it.
  • The tank on your water heater gave out and its contents are spreading through the room.
  • Water is still flowing and you cannot find or reach the shutoff valve.
  • Sewage has backed up, or the water is dark with a foul odor.
